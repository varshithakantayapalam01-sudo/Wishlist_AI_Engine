# Recommended Product Concept Brief

## Selected Concept: Verified Buyer Photo Highlight
**Targeting Opportunity**: Platform & Brand Trust

## Problem
When shoppers shortlist an item, they often abandon the purchase because they cannot trust the product quality or suspect reviews are fake (supported by 298 conversations).

## Target User
Users scrolling to reviews

## User Insight
High-intent users often abandon purchases because they lack specific confidence at the final decision moment. They don't need more inspiration; they need definitive validation.

## Jobs to Be Done
When I am ready to buy an unknown brand, I want to quickly verify its quality through authentic reviews, so I don't waste money on a poor product.

## Product Hypothesis
If we provide users scrolling to reviews with a verified buyer photo highlight, then they will provides immediate visual proof of actual product quality., because resolves the 'is quality good' uncertainty visually., resulting in an increase in 30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase User Conversion Rate.

## Proposed Experience
Surfaces user-uploaded photos to the top of the wishlist item page.

## User Flow
1. User wishlists item
2. Intent signal detected (User returns to app within 48h and views wishlist)
3. Relevant uncertainty identified based on item category
4. Decision-support intervention shown: Verified Buyer Photo Highlight
5. User resolves uncertainty
6. Add to cart & Purchase

## Why This Could Change Behavior
Provides immediate visual proof of actual product quality.

## Primary Metric
30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase User Conversion Rate

## Guardrails
Return Rate, Wishlist Abandonment Rate

## MVP vs Vision
**MVP**: Static rules-based trigger using historical aggregates.
**V2**: Personalized based on user's past browsing.
**Long-term Vision**: Fully contextual, predicting the exact friction point for that specific user and item.
