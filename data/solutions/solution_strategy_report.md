# Solution Strategy Report

## The Evidence-to-Experiment Narrative
This document traces the path from raw user feedback to a prioritized product experiment.

### 1. The User Evidence
In Phase 2, we analyzed 4,927 conversations and identified that users frequently abandon wishlists not due to lack of interest, but due to highly specific, unresolved uncertainties (Fit, Price, Quality).

### 2. The Opportunity
Phase 3 quantified these issues. We selected **Platform & Brand Trust** as the primary focus because it heavily impacts high-intent users. The data explicitly supported this as a major friction point.

### 3. The Solution Hypothesis
We hypothesize that: *If we provide users scrolling to reviews with a verified buyer photo highlight, then they will provides immediate visual proof of actual product quality., because resolves the 'is quality good' uncertainty visually., resulting in an increase in 30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase User Conversion Rate.*

### 4. The Experiment
We will test **Verified Buyer Photo Highlight**. This was chosen over AI-heavy alternatives because it requires significantly less implementation effort (Score: 1) while maintaining high Expected Impact (Score: 3).

### 5. The Business Metric
Success will be measured strictly by the **30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase User Conversion Rate**, ensuring we are driving true business value, not just feature engagement.

---
**Final Recommendation:**
We should build the MVP for **Verified Buyer Photo Highlight** immediately. It is a low-risk, high-evidence intervention targeting our most valuable user cohort.
